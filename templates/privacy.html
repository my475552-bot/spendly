Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

01Data We Collect

Spendly is built to track your spending, so most of what we hold is information you enter yourself. We collect only what the service needs to work.

  • Account details — the name and email address you register with, and a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you add.
  • Technical information — basic logs such as your browser type and the time of a request, kept to keep the service running and secure.

We do not ask for bank credentials, card numbers, or government identifiers, and you should never enter them into Spendly.

02How We Use Your Data

We use the information described above to:

  • Show you your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and improve how Spendly works.
  • Respond to you when you contact us for support.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records to build advertising profiles. We will only use your data for a new purpose if that purpose is compatible with the one it was collected for, or if you agree to it.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected by reasonable technical and organisational measures, including hashed passwords and access controls that limit who can reach it.

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual records or your whole account at any time; once deleted, data is removed from the live database, though copies may persist briefly in routine backups before they expire.

No system is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are encouraged to keep your own copies of anything you consider important.

04Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of outside providers to operate — for example, hosting infrastructure and the web fonts used to render this page. These providers may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, purely to deliver their part of the service.

We share your information with a third party only where it is needed to run the service, where you have asked us to, or where the law requires it. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

05Your Choices

You can view, correct, or delete the expense records in your account at any time from within the application, and you can close your account whenever you wish. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data or ask us to restrict how it is used.

06Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or would like to exercise any of the choices described above, please get in touch through the contact details provided within the application. We aim to respond to every request within a reasonable time.

We may update this Privacy Policy as Spendly evolves. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change.
